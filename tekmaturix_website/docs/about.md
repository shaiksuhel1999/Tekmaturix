# About

Tekmaturix is a place to collect ideas, innovations and knowledge, and to ask what comes
next.

The name comes from three parts. Tek (𒆳) for technology and knowledge. Matu (𒁍) for
courage and persistence. Rix, from Latin, for a leader. Roughly: understand things, keep
going, and build something useful for other people.

A few things the site tries to stick to:

- Big ideas tend to look impossible right before they look obvious.
- Be curious about anything, but ask for evidence.
- Knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied.
- The point of learning is to solve real problems for real people.
