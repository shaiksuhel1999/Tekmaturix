# Real-World Problems and Solutions

A running list of real problems, what people are trying, and roughly where things stand.
For the really big open questions there is a separate [Unsolved](../unsolved/index.md)
section.

## Climate change
Rising CO2 is warming the planet and driving worse weather and higher seas. What is being
tried: renewables (now the cheapest power we have ever had), electric cars,
[carbon removal](../unsolved/earth.md), [fusion](../unsolved/earth.md), replanting forests,
and better grid storage.

## Water scarcity
Around 2 billion people lack safe drinking water. What is being tried:
[cheaper desalination](../unsolved/earth.md), solar stills, fog nets, machines that pull
water from air, and smarter irrigation.

## Food waste
About a third of the food we grow is thrown away while millions go hungry. What is being
tried: better cold storage, demand forecasting, discounts on food near its expiry date,
apps that redistribute surplus, and coatings that keep produce fresh longer.

## Plastic pollution
Roughly 8 million tonnes of plastic reach the ocean every year, and microplastics are now
everywhere. What is being tried: [catching plastic at rivers](../unsolved/oceans.md) before
it reaches the sea, enzymes that break down plastic, compostable alternatives, and deposit
return schemes.

## Healthcare access
Billions of people cannot get affordable, decent care. What is being tried: telemedicine,
AI that can spot disease from a phone photo, drones that deliver medicine, cheap portable
devices, and trained community health workers.

## Traffic congestion
Cities lose huge amounts of time and fuel to gridlock. What is being tried: smarter traffic
lights, congestion charging, better public transport, remote work, and shared rides.

## Energy storage
Solar and wind come and go, so we need to store power for when they don't. What is being
tried: [better batteries](../unsolved/engineering.md), pumped hydro, green hydrogen, and
storing energy as heat or height.

## Affordable housing
Housing costs have outrun incomes in most cities. What is being tried:
[modular and 3D-printed homes](../unsolved/society.md), zoning reform, and mass timber for
faster mid-rise building.
